Beyond right answers

Two checks that look past the final answer: is the model's reasoning right (does it follow the water?), and does it know when the image cannot tell it? Every number below is read from results/trace and results/cloud.

  • Route check. The best model, Gemini 3.6 Flash, drew the right route on 4 of 7 images; Gemini 3.5 Flash 3 of 8; Gemini 3 Flash Preview 1 of 10; Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite 1 of 10; Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite 0 of 10; Qwen2-VL-2B 0 of 10; Qwen2.5-VL-3B 0 of 10. No reference line that ignores the streams passes: a straight line south passes 0 of 22.
  • Answer and reasoning come apart. 8 replies drew the right route but named the wrong villages; 7 named the right villages along a wrong route. Scoring only the final answer would have called 7 of these right and missed that 8 of them followed the water correctly.
  • When only the outfall moves, does the route move? Both routes of a before-and-after pair right: Gemini 3.6 Flash 1 of 3; Gemini 3.5 Flash 0 of 4; Gemini 3 Flash Preview 0 of 5; Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite 0 of 5; Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite 0 of 5; Qwen2-VL-2B 0 of 5; Qwen2.5-VL-3B 0 of 5.
  • Honesty check. Under a cloud that provably hides the answer, models gave a confident list of villages 34 times out of 42. Per model, said it cannot tell under the hidden cloud: Gemini 3.5 Flash 3 of 3; Gemini 3.6 Flash 4 of 4; Gemini 3 Flash Preview 1 of 5; Qwen2-VL-2B 0 of 10; Qwen2.5-VL-3B 0 of 10; Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite 0 of 5; Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite 0 of 5.
  • The decoy keeps it fair. The same cloud over streams that do not matter: models abstained there 0 times out of 41, so the abstentions under the hidden cloud are about what is hidden, not about clouds.
  • Right by luck still counts as wrong. 8 of the made-up lists happened to match the hidden truth: a guess the image cannot support, scored as one.

1 · Draw the water's path

The model is shown the same image as before and asked to trace the wastewater's route from outfall O as points on the image, in order, until the water leaves the area, and to list the villages it reaches. The simulation knows the whole route, so the drawn line is scored cell by cell: a route passes when at least 80% of the line lies within 4 cells (100 m) of the true path and the line covers at least 80% of it. The scorer is anchored by lines with known verdicts: the true path passes on every image, a straight line south passes on 0 of 22. Points are asked for as named x and y values; scored with the axes swapped, every model does worse (Qwen2-VL-2B 0.06 vs 0.01; Qwen2.5-VL-3B 0.03 vs 0.02; Gemini 3 Flash Preview 0.51 vs 0.10; Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite 0.24 vs 0.11; Gemini 3.5 Flash 0.65 vs 0.15; Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite 0.38 vs 0.09; Gemini 3.6 Flash 0.79 vs 0.16, mean F1 as asked vs swapped), so no model lost points to an axis mix-up.

2 · Can't tell: a cloud over the deciding stretch

Each item is one scene drawn three ways with the same question and prompt, where "cannot be determined" is always allowed: hidden, a cloud over the stretch where the route could leave by more than one channel; decoy, the same cloud (same shape) over streams that do not matter; clear, no cloud. Why the hidden version cannot be answered, for every item: rivers cannot cross without merging, so each channel leaving the cloud drains one unbroken stretch of its edge; the code finds a way to split the edge that sends the route out by another channel while every exit keeps exactly the water it carries (so every channel's width, and every visible pixel, stays the same) and every inflow stays higher than its exit. That other channel leads to other villages. Every item also passes pixel checks: the clear image is the published one, the cloud changes nothing outside itself and its shadow, its core is the same whatever lies beneath, and no village, label or stream symbol is under it.

In the tables below, any list given under the hidden cloud is marked red, even one that matches the truth: the image cannot support it, so it is a guess. Under the decoy and with no cloud, the list must be the right villages.
